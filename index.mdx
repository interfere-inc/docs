Interfere investigates problems in your app using errors, traces, logs, session replays, and the code behind a release.

Start in the Inbox. Open a problem to read the investigation, check its evidence, and see the users affected. Your team can assign an owner, discuss the findings, and follow the problem through recovery.

## Connect your app

Install the SDK for [Next.js](/sdk/next-js), [Vite and React](/sdk/vite), [TanStack Start](/sdk/tanstack-start), [NestJS](/sdk/nestjs), or [Node.js](/sdk/node). Connect [GitHub](/integrations/github) so investigations can reference your source code.

Each app you monitor is a **surface**. Your team's **workspace** contains those surfaces, their problems, and your integrations.

## Work from your own tools

Use [AI Agents](/integrations/plugins) or [MCP](/integrations/mcp) to investigate from your editor, the [CLI](/integrations/cli) to inspect problems from a terminal, and [Terraform](/integrations/terraform) to manage surfaces. The [TypeScript Platform SDK](/platform-sdk/typescript) supports custom API workflows.
