Work with our team to connect Interfere problems to Linear in a way that fits your triage process, whether you want to create issues, sync updates, or add context to existing work.

## Choose what to sync

Choose the workflow that fits your team:

- One-way sync sends Interfere problems to Linear.
- Two-way sync coordinates updates and triage across both tools.
- Issue enrichment adds Interfere context to issues already in Linear.

## Arrange setup

To set up or adjust the connection, contact [support@interfere.com](mailto:support@interfere.com) with the teams and issue statuses you use in Linear, how you triage issues, and what you want to sync.
