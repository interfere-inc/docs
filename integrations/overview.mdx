Open **Settings → Connections** to manage the services connected to your workspace.

## Source code

- [GitHub](/integrations/github) for repositories on `github.com`.
- [GitHub Enterprise Cloud](/integrations/github-enterprise-cloud) for enterprise hosts on `*.ghe.com`.
- [GitHub Enterprise Server](/integrations/github-enterprise-server) for self-hosted instances.

## Deployments

Connect [Vercel](/integrations/vercel) or [Cloudflare Workers](/integrations/cloudflare-workers) to associate deployments with surfaces and releases.

## Team workflows

Use [Slack](/integrations/slack) for channel updates and personal notifications. Connect [Linear](/integrations/linear) to sync problems or add investigation context to issues.

## Developer tools

Use the [MCP](/integrations/mcp), [CLI](/integrations/cli), [API](/integrations/api-keys), or [Terraform provider](/integrations/terraform) to work with Interfere from your own tools. [AI agent plugins](/integrations/plugins) combine the MCP connection with investigation guidance.

## Coming soon

AWS, Render, and Bitbucket appear in the product catalog as coming soon. They are not available to connect yet.
