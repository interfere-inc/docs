Connect Slack to send problem updates to a team channel and mentions or assignments to the people involved.

## Connect a workspace

Open [Slack settings](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/integrations/slack) and authorize Interfere in the Slack workspace you want to use.

## Route notifications to channels

Choose a channel for each category:

| Category      | Updates                                                    |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Problems      | New problems, regressions, and recoveries                  |
| Team activity | New surfaces, installs, and members                        |
| Collaboration | Mentions, assignments, reactions, and subscription updates |

For example, send problems to your engineering alerts channel and team activity to a separate workspace channel.

## Receive personal messages

Connect your Slack identity so Interfere can send mentions and assignments to you as direct messages. Choose personal delivery preferences in [Notifications](/product/notifications).

## Permissions

The connection requests permission to send messages, open direct messages, list channels, and match teammates by Slack identity and email. It also uses assistant and canvas permissions for its Slack views and problem previews.

## Reconnect

If the app is removed or access is revoked, reconnect from Slack settings. Existing channel routing is restored when you reconnect. Check that those channels still exist and are the destinations you want to use.
