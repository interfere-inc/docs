title: "Activity"
icon: "/icons/ui/bell.svg"
description: "Review problem updates and team activity across your workspace."
---

Activity shows workspace events in chronological order, including new problems, regressions, recoveries, surfaces coming online, and members joining.

Open a problem from an event to inspect it in the [problem view](/product/problems). Use the [Inbox](/product/inbox) to filter and triage problems.

For administrative changes, including who changed a setting or credential, use the [audit log](/product/audit-log).
