---

Open **Activity** for a chronological view of new problems, regressions, recoveries, surfaces coming online, and teammates joining. Select a problem event to open its investigation.

For example, after deploying a fix, check for a recovery event and open the problem to review subsequent occurrences. Use the Inbox when you need to filter or assign several problems.

Administrative changes, such as a role or credential change, appear in the [Audit log](/product/audit-log). Choose how updates reach you in [Notifications](/product/notifications).
