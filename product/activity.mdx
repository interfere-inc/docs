---

**Activity** shows workspace events in chronological order, including new problems, regressions, recoveries, surfaces coming online, and members joining.

Open a problem from an event to read its [investigation](/product/problems). Use the [Inbox](/product/inbox) to filter problems and assign work.

To find who changed a setting or credential, use the [Audit log](/product/audit-log). Configure delivery of updates in [Notifications](/product/notifications).
