**Companies** groups your app's users by customer account. Open a company to review its properties, associated users, and active problems.

## Investigate an account report

Find the account in **Companies**, then open **Problems** to see issues affecting its users. Select a problem to read the investigation and check the evidence.

Open **Users** to inspect an individual user's activity and sessions. This helps narrow an account-wide report to the people and actions involved.

Interfere associates identified users with companies using their work email domain. Personal email providers do not create a company association. Include the user's email when [identifying users](/sdk/identity). A company represents a customer of your app; your [workspace](/product/settings) contains the team investigating those customers' problems.
