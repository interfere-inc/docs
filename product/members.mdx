Manage workspace membership in [Team settings](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/team). Owners and administrators can invite teammates and manage access within their role's permissions.

## Invite a teammate

Select **Invite**, enter the teammate's email, and choose their role. Review outstanding invitations in Team settings.

To let colleagues join through your company email domain, configure [workspace domains](/product/settings#workspace-domains).

## Choose a role

| Role   | Access                                                               |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Member | Product features, including investigating problems                   |
| Admin  | Product features, workspace settings, member management, and billing |
| Owner  | Administrator access, plus workspace ownership transfer and deletion |

A member cannot manage workspace settings, membership, or billing. Administrators cannot change an owner's role. Role changes are limited by the acting member's own role.

Review membership changes in the [Audit log](/product/audit-log). For sign-in requirements and directory provisioning, see [Security](/product/security).
