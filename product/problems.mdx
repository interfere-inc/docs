Open a problem from the [Inbox](https://interfere.com/~/*) to see its description, affected users, evidence, and investigation timeline.

![Problem view showing details, timeline activity, evidence, and collaboration](/images/problem-detail.png)

## Read the investigation

The timeline records investigation updates and team discussion. Root-cause explanations include the relevant code and supporting facts. Follow the evidence links to inspect the records behind a conclusion. You can filter the timeline when you need a particular type of update.

An investigation may still be running or may fail before reaching a conclusion. Its current status remains visible in the Inbox. Recurring errors can trigger further investigation, and later releases can reopen a resolved problem as a regression.

## Inspect occurrences and traces

The findings list groups occurrences by day. Expand a day to inspect its records or collapse it to scan a longer time range.

Open a trace to review its spans and timing. Use **Jump to error** to locate the error in the span list, then inspect the stack trace for its call frames. Browser and platform details help distinguish failures that affect a particular environment.

## Watch affected sessions

Open the problem's sessions to see what affected users did. Filter by a visited URL to focus on a route, then open a replay. Opening a session from a problem keeps the session's other events available for context. When you jump to an error, replay starts seven seconds before it where recording data is available.

## Work the problem

- Assign a teammate with <kbd>A</kbd>.
- Set high, medium, or low priority with <kbd>P</kbd>.
- Edit the title or description to add context for your team.
- Comment on the timeline and mention teammates.
- Toggle your subscription with <kbd>S</kbd>.
- Dismiss a problem that does not need action, or archive it to remove it from your working view.

Use the feedback control to report an issue with the investigation. Include what is wrong and any evidence that would change the conclusion.

Interfere manages recovery and resolution as it observes new events. See [statuses](/concepts#statuses) for the lifecycle.

## Notifications and editor access

Configure notifications in [Alerts](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/alerts). Connect [Slack](/integrations/slack) for team notifications, or use the [MCP server](/integrations/mcp) to inspect the same workspace from a coding agent.
