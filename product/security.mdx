Manage how members sign in and which networks can access your workspace. For security reports and certifications, visit [Interfere security](https://interfere.com/security).

## Sign in

Sign in with an email one-time code or a passkey. Add a passkey in [Profile settings](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/profile) after your first sign-in. Passkeys can use device biometrics or a hardware security key.

## Single sign-on

Connect a SAML 2.0 identity provider to manage team sign-in. Contact [support@interfere.com](mailto:support@interfere.com) with your provider to arrange setup.

Existing members link their Interfere account to the identity provider when they next sign in. New members who join through SSO receive Member access; an administrator can change their role afterward.

When SSO is required, members must use the identity provider. Email and passkey sign-in are disabled. SCIM directory sync can provision and deprovision accounts from your identity provider.

## Roles and permissions

Assign Owner, Admin, or Member access in [Members and roles](/product/members).

## Network access

Enterprise workspaces can restrict dashboard and API access in [Security settings](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/security). Changing rules requires workspace authentication management permission.

Select **Add a network**, enter an IPv4 or IPv6 address, CIDR range, or ASN, and add a description. You can configure up to 100 rules.

With no rules, authenticated members can access the workspace from any network. Once rules exist, connections must match an allowed network. An ASN permits the network operator's entire network, including other customers. Use a narrower IP range when you need access limited to your own network.

If a connection is blocked, connect through an allowed company network or VPN. Ask an administrator to review the rules if your expected network is missing. Members still need workspace permissions after passing the network check.

Review changes in the [Audit log](/product/audit-log).
