Manage network access and sign-in for your workspace. For certifications and reports, see [Compliance](/product/compliance).

## IP ranges

Interfere publishes its owned IPv4 and IPv6 ranges at [interfere.com/ips.json](https://interfere.com/ips.json). The JSON contains a `prefixes` array with `ipv4Prefix` and `ipv6Prefix` entries in CIDR notation. Use this feed when maintaining firewall rules for Interfere-owned networks.

These ranges identify Interfere-owned address space. They do not cover every request from Interfere services, including services running on shared infrastructure. Before restricting an integration to these ranges, confirm its source addresses with [support@interfere.com](mailto:support@interfere.com).

## Network access

<EnterpriseBadge />

Enterprise workspaces can whitelist the networks allowed to reach their dashboard and API in [Settings → Security](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/security). Editing rules requires workspace authentication management permission.

Select **Add a network**, enter an IPv4 or IPv6 address, CIDR range, or ASN, and add a description. For example, a CIDR entry can identify an office network and an ASN can identify a network operator. You can keep up to 100 rules.

With no rules, authenticated members can reach the workspace from any network. Once rules are configured, connections must match an allowed network. An ASN allows that operator's entire network, including other customers, so use a narrower IP range when appropriate.

If access is blocked, connect through an allowed company network or VPN. Ask a workspace administrator to review the rules if the expected network is missing. Workspace permissions still apply after a connection passes the network check.

Review administrative changes in the [audit log](/product/audit-log).

## Signing in

Email and passkeys work for everyone. Organizations can add single sign-on through their own identity provider.

  </Card>

## Single sign-on (SSO)

Organizations can have members sign in through their own identity provider (IdP) with SAML 2.0, so access follows the accounts and groups you already manage. Interfere works with any SAML 2.0 provider, including Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, OneLogin, and JumpCloud.

Our team sets up SSO with you; it isn't self-serve. To turn it on for your organization, reach out at [support@interfere.com](mailto:support@interfere.com), open [support settings](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/support), and tell us which identity provider you use. We'll exchange the SAML details and enable it together.

### How SSO behaves

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Linking existing accounts" icon="/icons/ui/link.svg">
    After SSO is on, current members link their existing Interfere account to your IdP the next time they sign in. It's a one-time step for each person and keeps their history and settings intact.
  </Accordion>

<Accordion title="New members" icon="/icons/ui/user-plus.svg">
  Someone signing in through SSO for the first time joins with standard member access. An
  administrator can change their role afterward.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Requiring SSO" icon="/icons/ui/key.svg">
  You can require everyone in your organization to sign in through SSO. While it's required, email
  and passkey sign-in are turned off, so your identity provider is the only way in.
</Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Directory sync (SCIM)" icon="/icons/ui/sync.svg">
    With SCIM, your identity provider provisions and deprovisions accounts for you. When someone joins or leaves the relevant group in your IdP, their Interfere access follows, so off-boarding isn't a manual step.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Roles and permissions

Interfere uses role-based access control. Every member has a role that sets what they can see and change, from full administration down to read-only access. Manage roles from your workspace's [team settings](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/team).
