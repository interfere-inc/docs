In [Settings → Security](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/security), use **Access control → Sign-in methods** to choose the providers available to users associated with your verified domains.

## Choose sign-in methods

With permission to manage workspace authentication, you can enable or disable Google, GitHub, and Vercel in this menu.

<Note>
  Turning off every selectable provider disables Google, GitHub, and Vercel sign-in. Email codes and
  passkeys remain enabled, so this does not restrict sign-in to enterprise SSO.
</Note>

## Connect an enterprise identity provider

To use your company's SAML or OIDC provider, set up [Single sign-on](/product/security/sso). Your active connection appears as **Enterprise SSO** in the sign-in methods menu and can be managed from the **Single sign-on** row.

If you also want the provider to manage users and groups, configure [Directory sync](/product/security/scim) alongside SSO.

## Verify your company domain

[Verified domains](/product/workspace-domains) associate your team's email domain with the workspace and control enrollment. A domain managed by an enterprise identity provider displays that relationship in Security.
