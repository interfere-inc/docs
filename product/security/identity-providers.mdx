Open [Settings → Security](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/security), then **Access control → Sign-in methods**. This setting controls the identity providers available to users associated with your verified domains.

## Choose sign-in methods

The menu lets you enable or disable Google, GitHub, and Vercel sign-in. Changing these choices requires permission to manage workspace authentication.

Email codes and passkeys are retained by the settings flow and are not toggles in this menu. Enabling an enterprise SSO connection does not, by itself, disable them.

<Note>
  Ask support about your required authentication policy before relying on an SSO-only rollout. The
  sign-in methods menu does not provide an “enforce SSO only” switch.
</Note>

## Connect an enterprise identity provider

Use [Single sign-on](/product/security/sso) for a company-managed SAML or OIDC connection. Once an active connection is available, **Enterprise SSO** appears in the sign-in methods menu. Manage the connection in the **Single sign-on** row.

If your goal is to synchronize users or groups, configure [Directory sync](/product/security/scim) as well. Authentication and membership provisioning are separate controls.

## Verify your company domain

[Verified domains](/product/workspace-domains) associate your team's email domain with the workspace and control enrollment. A domain managed by an enterprise identity provider displays that relationship in Security.
