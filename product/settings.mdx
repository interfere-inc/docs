Your workspace contains your team's surfaces, problems, integrations, and access settings. Open [Workspace settings](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/workspace) to manage its name and defaults.

The workspace slug appears in its URL. For `interfere.com/~/acme`, use `acme` in the CLI, API, and Terraform provider.

## Data residency

View your workspace's **Data residency** in [Workspace settings](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/workspace). Workspaces currently use **Unrestricted**, which does not restrict storage or processing to a specific region. See [Data residency](/product/data-residency) for availability and scope.

## Workspace domains

Configure [verified domains](/product/workspace-domains) in **Settings → Security** to control how people with your team's email domain join. This feature requires Enterprise.

## Team access

Invite teammates and change roles in [Members and roles](/product/members). Configure [SSO and network access](/product/security) in Security, and review administrative changes in the [Audit log](/product/audit-log).

## Personal settings

Update your account details and passkeys in [Profile](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/profile). Choose notification channels in [Notifications](/product/notifications).

## Billing

Open [Billing](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/billing) to manage your plan and invoices.

## Support access

Support access lets the Interfere team temporarily access your workspace to troubleshoot with you, using the same access you have. It is off by default.

Enable it in [Support settings](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/support) when working with support. Choose a duration from one hour to one week. Access expires automatically, and you can revoke it earlier.

Contact [support@interfere.com](mailto:support@interfere.com) for help.
