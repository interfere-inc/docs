A surface is an app you monitor, such as a web frontend or backend service. Give separately deployed apps their own surfaces.

## Create a surface

Follow [Create a surface](/product/surfaces/create) to name your app, choose its framework, and connect the SDK.

## Check incoming events

Open the surface in [Surfaces](https://interfere.com/~/*/surfaces) and inspect its event chart. New surfaces wait for their first event; after capture starts, the chart shows event volume over time.

If you have deployed but the chart is empty, check [No events arriving](/sdk/troubleshooting).

## Keys

The public key identifies the surface receiving telemetry and can ship in browser code. The secret API key registers releases and uploads source maps from your build pipeline. Keep it in your server or CI environment.

Open the surface's key settings to manage these credentials. See [API keys](/integrations/api-keys) for workspace automation keys.

## Environments and releases

An environment labels traffic from a deployment, such as `production` or `staging`. Use environment filters when reviewing problems so you can focus on the deployment you are investigating.

A release identifies a version of your code. Open the surface's **Releases** tab to review its environments and deployment times. See [Releases](/product/surfaces/releases) for how releases and source maps help you investigate a problem.

Use [Terraform](/integrations/terraform) to manage surface names and framework types in infrastructure configuration.
