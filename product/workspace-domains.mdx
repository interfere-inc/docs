---
title: "Workspace domains"
description: "Control how teammates with your email domain join the workspace."
---

A workspace domain is your team's email domain, such as `acme.com`. It controls who can join your workspace: people with a verified email at that domain can be invited automatically or can request access, so you don't have to add colleagues one by one.

This is your company email domain, not the web address your product runs on. Free email providers (like gmail.com) and IP addresses aren't allowed.

You add one domain when you create the workspace, and you can add more later. Each domain has its own join setting:

- **Manual**. Invite people yourself.
- **Automatic invitation**. Invite anyone with a matching email automatically.
- **Suggested**. Show people with a matching email a prompt to request access.

Remove a domain in [workspace settings](https://interfere.com/~/*/settings/workspace) and joining returns to manual invites.
